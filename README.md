# ✈️ United Airlines — Analytics & Strategic Case Study

> A business analytics case study focused on **United Airlines' performance, strategic growth, COVID-19 recovery, and long-term business strategy**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an analytical and strategic study of **United Airlines**, examining the company's business performance and strategic direction across different periods.

The case study focuses on understanding how United Airlines responded to major challenges, particularly the disruption caused by the **COVID-19 pandemic**, while also examining its recovery, business growth, and strategic initiatives.

The project combines **business research, data interpretation, strategic analysis, and presentation of findings** to develop a structured understanding of United Airlines' business environment.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this case study are:

* Analyze United Airlines' business and operational performance.
* Understand the impact of COVID-19 on the airline industry and United Airlines.
* Study United Airlines' recovery and growth after the pandemic.
* Examine the company's strategic initiatives and business decisions.
* Identify important trends and factors affecting airline performance.
* Understand how strategic decisions can support long-term growth.
* Present analytical findings in a clear and structured manner.
